# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and clear. Healthy disagreement is welcome; harassment, abuse, intimidation, and personal attacks are not.

Examples of encouraged behavior:

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and security-sensitive information.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without consent
- Sharing exploit details or secrets in public channels

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in restricted participation.

For organization-level concerns, contact @wcypierre through GitHub. Repository maintainers may define more specific enforcement procedures for their own projects.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to participation in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces for this organization unless a repository provides its own policy.
