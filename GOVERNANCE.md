# Governance

## Overview

Repositories in this organization are maintained by @wcypierre and any maintainers explicitly granted access to individual repositories.

## Decision Making

Decisions are usually made by the maintainer responsible for the affected repository. Maintainers should prefer small, reversible changes and clear rationale.

For larger changes, maintainers should use issues, pull requests, or design notes to document the problem, tradeoffs, and verification plan.

## Roles

- Owner: manages organization-level policy, repository access, and final escalation.
- Maintainer: reviews, merges, releases, and triages within assigned repositories.
- Contributor: proposes issues, pull requests, documentation, or improvements.

## Repository Overrides

Individual repositories may document their own governance, release, or review process. Repository-specific guidance takes precedence over this default file.

## Security and Conduct

Security reports follow `SECURITY.md`. Conduct concerns follow `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` unless a repository provides a stricter local policy.
